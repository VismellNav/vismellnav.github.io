BEYOND WHAT THE CAMERA SEES

An odor source is more
than a visual target.

VismellNav combines visual context with distributed olfactory sensing for odor-source navigation. The demonstrations explore changing obstacle layouts, a natural odor source, and targets with matching visual appearance.

PROJECT OVERVIEW

Bring the senses together.

Visual information provides scene context. Distributed olfactory measurements provide complementary odor cues. Together with robot state, these observations support navigation.

01 / SENSE

Complementary observations

RGB history, four leg-mounted electronic noses and proprioception capture the scene, odor measurements and robot state.

02 / FUSE

Observation-dependent weighting

Modality-specific features are projected into a shared latent space and combined through adaptive gating.

03 / ACT

Flow-matching control

The fused representation conditions action-chunk generation for closed-loop navigation.

SENSING HARDWARE

A robot that sees and smells.

Distributed olfactory sensing

A quadruped platform integrates an RGB camera and leg-mounted electronic noses, bringing spatially distributed odor measurements into the navigation system.
